# About us

## The Autonomy Notebook

The Autonomy Notebook is a hands-on collection of knowledge about autonomous systems, written from the workbench and the research lab. It brings together practical tutorials, engineering tips and research insights on robotics, autonomous vehicles, computer vision and machine learning.

Whether you are a seasoned engineer, a curious enthusiast, or someone new to the field, these notes are meant to make complex robotic systems easier to understand and build. Let's learn, build and innovate together!
